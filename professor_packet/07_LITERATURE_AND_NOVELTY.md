## Contribution wording allowed now
> "Under an idealised Q2 phase-oracle model, we characterise BV key recovery against input-dependent noise via the secret-shifted Walsh spectrum. We define a spectral-gap criterion, measure it on finite control families, and exhibit structural ambiguity (zero-gap) cases. A published deterministic-LPN construction has **not yet** been evaluated."
