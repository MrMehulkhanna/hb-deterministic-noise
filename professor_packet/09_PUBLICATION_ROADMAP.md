# Publication roadmap (conditional; no acceptance is implied)

1. **Gate:** Group E construction result + complete novelty search (open items 1–4).
2. **Software release:** GitHub repository, then tag `v0.2.0-controls`, then a Zenodo DOI for that release.
3. **Then:** tag `v0.3.0-construction` after Group E, and `v1.0.0` after the final review.
4. **Preprint:** IACR ePrint only if the construction result gives a substantive cryptographic finding. Check the current ePrint rules at submission. An ePrint posting is not peer review.
5. **Venues:** shortlist 2–4 only after the result is known, matched to the contribution (e.g. a post-quantum or applied-cryptography conference, a quantum-crypto workshop). CFPs to be checked at that time.
6. **ORCID:** link the DOI and preprint after they exist.
