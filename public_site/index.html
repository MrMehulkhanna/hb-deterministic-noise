Spectral leakage and repeated Bernstein–Vazirani key recovery under deterministic, input-dependent noise

Status: in progress. Control experiments verified; the published deterministic-LPN construction experiment is pending. No security or novelty claim is made.

Under an idealised Q2 phase oracle, the BV output distribution equals the secret-shifted squared Walsh spectrum of the noise sign function, so P(secret) = (1−2τ)² and recovery depends on the spectral gap Δ.
